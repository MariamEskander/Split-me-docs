The short version

Billy has no accounts and no servers of its own. It collects no personal data, contains no analytics and no advertising, and does not track you. Your bills stay on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Everything you create in the app — bills, items, the people you split with, saved groups, and your Essentials list — is stored in a database on your device only. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Receipt photos

When you scan a receipt, the image is read by Apple's on-device text recogniser, which runs locally on your iPhone. The photo is not uploaded and is not saved. Only the items you confirm on the review screen are kept, together with the recognised text of that receipt so a bill can be re-checked later.

Location

Location is optional and used for two distinct purposes. Nothing about your location is stored on any server, and nothing is sent to us — we have no server to send it to.

  • While using the app — to suggest nearby restaurants and cafés when naming a bill. This runs a search through Apple Maps, so your approximate location and the search text are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Always — used only for the Essentials reminder. You set a home location, and iOS tells the app when you leave the area around it so a reminder can be shown. The comparison happens on your device. This permission is never required: without it, the reminder becomes a daily one at a time you choose.

You can withdraw either permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, and the rest of the app continues to work.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your own device. There is no push server, and no device token is collected or transmitted.

Network requests

The app makes exactly two kinds of network request, neither of which carries personal information:

  • Exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com — a request for the current rate table. No identifiers are sent.
  • Place search through Apple Maps, when you ask for nearby places, as described above.

Diagnostics

Billy uses Luciq, a mobile monitoring service, to find out when the app crashes or misbehaves. It sends crash reports, performance measurements, and any bug report you choose to submit yourself. This is diagnostic information about the app — not the contents of your bills.

To make a problem reproducible, the app records the steps you took before it happened, with screenshots of the screens you were on. This applies to bug reports you submit and to crashes. Because this app's screens show bills, those screenshots can include item names, amounts, and the names you gave the people you split with.

None of it is linked to your identity, and none of it is used to track you or shown to advertisers.

Exactly what is collected

These are the same three categories declared on this app's App Store privacy card, and they are the only data the app collects:

  • Crash data — the stack trace and device state when the app stops unexpectedly, plus the steps and screenshots leading up to it.
  • Performance data — how long screens take to appear, how long network requests take, and app launch and hang timings.
  • Other diagnostic data — the app version, iOS version, device model, and any bug report you write and send yourself.

All three are not linked to your identity and not used for tracking. There is no account, so there is no identity to link them to.

The diagnostics are processed by Luciq on our behalf as a service provider, under their privacy policy. They are not sold, and not shared with anyone else.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, marketing or behavioural analytics. The only data leaving the app is the crash and performance diagnostics described above.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of data. There is no data to sell.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, email lists, or contact-list access.

Sharing a bill

When you share a breakdown, the app hands plain text to the iOS share sheet, and you choose where it goes — WhatsApp, Messages, or anywhere else. The app does not send anything itself, and keeps no record of what you shared.

Children

Billy is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy
